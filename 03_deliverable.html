Target Capacity Statement

Under the defined baseline workload, Agent Hub supports approximately X concurrent active agents / Y sustainable QPM while maintaining P95 ≤ Z seconds. A minimum infrastructure unit provides approximately A QPM capacity, while infrastructure scaling achieves approximately B% efficiency. The primary observed capacity indicator is Resource C.

Management Decision View

At completion, management should be able to answer:“If Agent Hub usage grows to N concurrent agents / N QPM, approximately how much infrastructure capacity will we require while maintaining the required service level?”

Overall Success Criterion

The assessment is successful when the test evidence provides a defensible connection between demand (concurrent agents and QPM), service quality (response time and reliability), and required infrastructure capacity, with sufficient evidence to support future sizing decisions.
